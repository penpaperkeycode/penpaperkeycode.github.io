{%- comment -%} Renders one short Markdown string inline (no

wrapper) and opens its external links in a new tab. A YAML slip such as - 2026: Promoted ... (missing double quotes) arrives here as a one-entry hash; it is turned back into "2026: Promoted ..." instead of {2026=>...}. Usage: {% include home/inline.html text=some_value %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign raw = include.text -%} {%- assign pair = raw | first -%} {%- if raw.text == nil and pair and pair.size == 2 -%}{%- capture raw -%}{{ pair[0] }}: {{ pair[1] }}{%- endcapture -%}{%- endif -%} {%- capture newtab -%} (opens in a new tab){%- endcapture -%} {{- raw | markdownify | remove: "

" | remove: "
